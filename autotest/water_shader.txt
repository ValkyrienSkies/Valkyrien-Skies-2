# Does world fluid still get the shaderpack's water program once it is re-meshed into the cull layer?
#
# Ship fluid culling moves world water out of the translucent chunk layer into AIR_CULL_RENDER_TYPE
# (vanilla) / AIR_POCKET_PASS (Sodium). Under a shaderpack, water is supposed to be drawn by
# gbuffers_water, which is what gives it waves and reflections. If the re-meshed layer instead lands on
# gbuffers_terrain, the surface goes flat and still — visible immediately on a large open pool.
#
# Run this twice with Underwater.enableWaterCulling flipped in valkyrienskies-client.toml. Culling off
# is the control: water takes the stock translucent pass. The two sets of screenshots should be
# indistinguishable. Any difference in the water surface is the cull layer losing the water program.
world autotest
wait-level
wait 100
cmd gamemode creative
cmd time set noon
cmd gamerule doDaylightCycle false

# A wide, deep pool: big enough that wave animation and reflections are obvious.
cmd fill 62 -60 62 138 -57 138 minecraft:stone
wait 10
cmd fill 64 -59 64 136 -57 136 minecraft:water
wait 30

# Just above the surface, looking out across the water at a grazing angle, where a pack's
# normals and reflections show up most strongly.
cmd tp @s 100 -56 70 0 8
wait 60
screenshot water_surface_a
wait 20
screenshot water_surface_b

# Steeper angle: shows the wave normals rather than the reflection.
cmd tp @s 100 -52 100 0 50
wait 40
screenshot water_down
quit
