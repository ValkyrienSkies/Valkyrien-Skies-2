# Is the shaderpack's water VERTEX shader running on the cull layer?
#
# Wave displacement is a vertex-stage effect: gbuffers_water moves the surface geometry up and down.
# Nothing in the fragment stage can fake it, so it is the honest test for "is the pack's vertex shader
# being applied", as opposed to just getting its colour.
#
# The camera sits a little above the surface looking almost flat across a long stretch of water, which
# is where displacement reads most clearly — an undisplaced surface is a razor-straight line against
# the sky, a displaced one has a visibly broken, moving silhouette. Frames are spaced out so the
# crests travel between them; a still surface across all four means the vertex shader is not running.
#
# Run once with Underwater.enableWaterCulling true (fluid on AIR_CULL_RENDER_TYPE / AIR_POCKET_PASS)
# and once false (stock translucent layer). Matching silhouettes and matching motion mean the cull
# layer is getting the same vertex program as ordinary world water.
world autotest
wait-level
wait 100
cmd gamemode spectator
cmd time set noon
cmd gamerule doDaylightCycle false
cmd gamerule sendCommandFeedback false

cmd fill 62 -60 62 138 -57 138 minecraft:stone
wait 10
cmd fill 64 -59 64 136 -57 136 minecraft:water
wait 40

# Just above the surface at the near rim, looking down the long axis of the pool.
cmd tp @s 100 -57.3 66 0 1
wait 60
screenshot vertex_f0
wait 15
screenshot vertex_f1
wait 15
screenshot vertex_f2
wait 15
screenshot vertex_f3
quit
