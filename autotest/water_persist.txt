# Does placed world water survive, and is it visible at all?
#
# Both arms of the shader A/B came out identical and waterless, which points at the scene rather than
# the render path. This isolates that: shoot the pool immediately after filling, again after it has had
# time to settle, and from inside the volume where water is unmistakable. If the early shot has water
# and the late one does not, something is draining it (playtest's flooding ships a world-fluid outflow
# manager and a source-withdrawal handler). If none of them ever show water, the fill is not landing
# where the camera is looking and the scene is simply wrong.
world autotest
wait-level
wait 100
cmd gamemode creative
cmd time set noon
cmd gamerule doDaylightCycle false

cmd fill 62 -60 62 138 -57 138 minecraft:stone
wait 10
cmd fill 64 -59 64 136 -57 136 minecraft:water

# Straight away, before anything has had a chance to drain it.
cmd tp @s 100 -52 100 0 50
wait 5
screenshot persist_immediate
wait 60
screenshot persist_after_3s
wait 200
screenshot persist_after_13s

# Inside the volume: underwater is unambiguous regardless of surface shading.
cmd tp @s 100 -58 100 0 0
wait 40
screenshot persist_submerged
quit
